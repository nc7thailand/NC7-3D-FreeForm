### What is BROKEN (the SVG "frame" bug)

The SVG diagnostic (`scripts/silhouette-theta90.svg`, generated by
`scripts/gen-svg.mjs`) shows a **straight "frame" around the shadow** — flat top,
bottom, and left edges — plus horizontally-stacked segment artifacts.

**Root cause (confirmed):**
1. `wirePath.js` → `processWireProfile` applies `clampProfileToStock`, which
   clamps `u` to `[−(halfW+lo), 0]` and `v` to `[0, H]` — this pins contour
   points onto the foam-block rectangle, creating the "frame" edges.
2. (Previously) `orderTopDown` sorted the whole closed loop by height, destroying
   loop topology into a zig-zag "comb". That sort has been **removed**, but the
   frame from `clampProfileToStock` remains.

## 2. THE 5-STAGE ARCHITECTURE (agreed spec)

The silhouette work must follow this exact, physical-model-accurate pipeline.
The silhouette is **only the shadow outline — one closed loop**. No fill, no
frame, no block boundary, no sort.

1. **Stage 1 — Closed silhouette loop** (per rotation angle θ)
   One closed polyline, the shadow edge only. Emitted exactly as `d3-contour`
   produces it. No fill, no frame, no clamp, no sort.

2. **Stage 2 — Vector boolean split**
   Split the closed loop at the rotation axis (the "paper middle line").
   → Left half + right half. This is a *vector boolean operation*, **not** a
   `filter(u ≤ 0)` and **not** a sort.

3. **Stage 3 — Open the loop** (closed → open vector)
   Cut bottom at **Cutout Offset Bottom**; cut top at **Min Axis Distance**.

4. **Stage 4 — Extend with safe points**
   Top: extend at Safe Point Angle Top (default 90°) by Safe Point Offset Top.
   Bottom: extend at Safe Point Angle Bottom (default 0°) by Safe Point Offset
   Bottom.

5. **Stage 5 — G-code**
   Wrap with lead-in, retract, and index moves.

---

## 3. PRINCIPLES (non-negotiable)

- **No sort by height** — sorting a closed loop breaks its topology.
- **No clamp to the block boundary** — this creates the "frame".
- **No frame** — the shadow must not have straight block-boundary edges.
- **No Chaikin smoothing** — must match DevFoam's deterministic (jagged-if-coarse)
  output. `d3-contour`'s linear interpolation along grid edges is *fine* (it is
  the canonical marching squares result, not post-smoothing).
- **Silhouette = ONE closed loop** exactly as `d3-contour` emits it.
